## Scope

The Wrenfield Library one-page site (React app in this folder). Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job

A neighbour checking whether the library is open now, what's on this month, and how to get a card. Real audience: internship reviewers checking accessible layout. Action: apply for a library card. Proof: real opening hours, a live open/closed status, four dated events. No invented stats, reviews or partners.

## Constraints

WCAG 2.2 AA, verified. Front-end only form. Fictional details stay obviously fictional.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Wrenfield's date-due slip. Every fact a visitor needs is a date or a time, and the library stamps it in ink. It refuses the municipal default of a shelf photo, a search bar and three quick-link tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Goldenrod card-stock field. Slip-white ruled cards with red printed rules and DATE DUE caps. Violet stamp-pad ink for every date and status, with fresher ink darker. Barlow Condensed for stamps and headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for typed tickets. At night the field becomes the ink pad (deep violet) and the ink turns goldenrod.

STORY: The visitor sees at once whether the library is open, then what's on, then how to get a card. They believe everyone is welcome. They apply and get an ISSUED stamp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five of twelve columns: H1 "Your library on Harbor Road", a one-line lede, the violet Get a card button. Right seven: a tall DATE DUE slip. A live OPEN NOW or CLOSED NOW stamp owns its top third at display scale, rotated about 3 degrees; the week's hours are ruled below it; the closure note is in red ink. The primary action sits above the fold at 1280 by 720.

FORM: Date-due slip and rubber stamp, position 3 on my grounded list, seed key 93f9943f. Signature interaction: stamps press onto paper (live status on load, ISSUED on card submit), shown instantly under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The ISSUED stamp landing on the borrower card after a successful application.
