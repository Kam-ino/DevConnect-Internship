Final project · A service with a job running behind it

Spending Wrapped

Upload a year of spending as a CSV and get a year-in-review. The upload returns straight away, and a separate worker process does the import in the background. You can kill that worker in the middle of a job and watch it recover with exactly the same result.

3. Background import

    
The worker saves a checkpoint after every 50 rows. Press Crash the worker while it runs: the process gets SIGKILL, the supervisor records it and starts a new worker, and the job picks up from the last checkpoint.
